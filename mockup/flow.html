Import trades

Bring in your broker's trade history instead of typing it. Nothing is ever added twice.

What to import

CSV or Excel (.xlsx) only. Equity trades only.

Zerodha: P&L report (Console, Reports, P&L), not the Tax P&L. It has no trade dates, so buys are dated the report start and sells the report end. Download one financial year at a time.

Groww: Stocks order history (Reports).

Upload trades

Choose the broker and upload its report. CSV and Excel files both work. Uploading the same file again, or an overlapping date range, never adds a trade twice.

Sync holdings

Update your holdings from your Zerodha account.

Zerodha

Compares your Zerodha holdings with this app and fills in the difference: extra shares become a buy at Zerodha's average price, missing shares become a sale at the last price. Both are marked estimated. To disconnect Zerodha, go to Settings.
